CHAPTER 01 · A NEW BEGINNING

Leaving Home

A small story about choosing a new direction.

After years of studying in her hometown, Lily decided to pursue a new opportunity abroad.

She knew that moving to another country would not be easy. She had to adapt to a different culture, manage her time carefully, and overcome many challenges.

At first, everything seemed unfamiliar. Slowly, she established new friendships and learned how to maintain a balance between study and personal life.

She still felt nervous sometimes, but each small experience helped her become more independent. She began to see uncertainty not as a wall, but as an opportunity to grow.

CHAPTER 02 · THE FIRST WEEK

Finding Her Place

During her first week, Lily had to adjust to a new schedule. The small differences in daily life were sometimes confusing, but she slowly became more confident.

One afternoon, a classmate invited her to a student club. Lily was unsure at first, but she decided to participate. By the end of the meeting, she had discovered that making friends was easier when she stopped worrying about making mistakes.
